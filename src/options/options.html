Malicious URL Detector — Options

Sensitivity

Sensitivity moves the score thresholds. Strict flags sooner (more warnings, more false positives); lenient flags later.

Google Safe Browsing (optional reputation lookup)

Without a key, URL Sentinel works offline with heuristics only and no data leaves your device. With a key, every visited URL is also checked against Google Safe Browsing — which means the URLs you visit are sent to Google's API. A match overrides the verdict to Malicious. Get a free key from the Google Cloud Console (Safe Browsing API).

Custom lists

One entry per line — a domain (example.com, matches subdomains too) or part of a URL. The allowlist always wins over every other check, including your own blocklist.
